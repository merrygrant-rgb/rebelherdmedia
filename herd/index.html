The Rebel Herd Initiative · Applications November 16–28, 2026

Independent businesses shouldn't have to succeed alone.

Independent Businesses. Collective Strength. In the first quarter of 2027, Rebel Herd Media will build a custom three-page website for five U.S. small businesses or 501(c)(3) nonprofits, selected on merit, with a $0 design fee — on a domain and a hosting account in each organization's own name.

Apply by November 28What selected organizations receive

Why we're doing this

A strong business community works the way a herd does: its members share what they know, look out for one another, and understand that one member doing well tends to help the rest. That is the idea behind our name, and this initiative is our first deliberate investment in it.

We are not simply handing out websites. We are putting our professional time into helping five organizations take more ownership of their own digital presence. This is not a membership, a partnership, or a referral arrangement, and nobody who applies owes us anything.

— Merry Grant, Founder & Principal

What selected organizations receive

  • One custom, three-page, static-first website — typically Home, About or Services, and Contact — built around your organization rather than a template.
  • Mobile-responsive design and documented accessibility practices.
  • A domain and hosting account in your name. You register the domain and open the hosting account yourself, with our help; we are never the owner of either.
  • A basic contact form.
  • One consolidated revision round. When your working preview is ready, we send it with a written deadline; you have seven calendar days from that notice to send all your changes in one list, and we make them.
  • A practical handoff explaining your account access and what is yours to look after from then on.
  • A $0 Rebel Herd Media design fee.

Our commitment is finite: up to 15 hours of professional time per website, 75 hours in total across the five.

What you supply: your own text, photographs, branding materials, and the business information we need. Light editing of your content is included; full copywriting is not.

Outside the pro bono scope: e-commerce, custom booking systems, complex integrations, additional pages, and ongoing maintenance.

What you pay for: your own domain registration and renewal, hosting, and any optional third-party services you choose.

Design credit: each website carries a small, discreet "Designed by Rebel Herd Media" credit in the footer.

Domains, hosting and what things cost

You don't have to figure out the technology alone. Here is the plain-language version of what you will hold and what it costs to keep.

Domain name
Your web address (yourbusiness.com). You register it with a domain registrar in your own name and renew it yearly — typically $10–$25 a year for a .com. We help you choose a name that is easy to say on the phone and check that it is available.
Hosting
Where the website files live. We set the site up in a hosting account that belongs to you. The service we use currently offers a free plan that suits most small sites; plans and limits are set by the hosting company and can change, and a paid tier is your choice if you ever need one.
Email
A you@yourbusiness.com address is separate from the website and usually $6–$8 a month per person from an email provider. Optional; many organizations start with the address they already have.
Forms and extras
The basic contact form is included. Anything beyond that — booking, payments, newsletters — is a third-party service with its own costs, and we tell you before you sign up for one.
The files
The website is made of standard web files, handed over to you. Another web developer can work on it later; it does not depend on a proprietary website builder.

Who can apply, and how we choose

Open to independently owned small businesses and 501(c)(3) nonprofits based in the United States with ten or fewer employees, whose owner or director is 18 or older. One application per organization. Government agencies and public school districts are not eligible for this initial round. Current clients and family of Rebel Herd Media are not eligible. No purchase is required, and nothing you buy or don't buy from us affects selection.

The five organizations are selected by Merry Grant on merit, using the criteria below. Every application is read in full, the review is documented, and nothing is decided by a random drawing or a random tiebreaker.

Mission & story — 35%
What you do, why you started, and who it helps.
Demonstrated website need — 30%
How much a working website would change things for you, and whether you have one now.
Readiness to collaborate — 20%
Whether you can supply your text and photos in the first quarter of 2027.
Suitability for a three-page scope — 15%
Whether a three-page site is the right size for what you need right now.

Dates and next steps

  • Applications open Monday, November 16, 2026.
  • Applications close Saturday, November 28, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern.
  • Review on Sunday, November 29. Selected organizations are contacted privately by email.
  • Introductions begin Monday, November 30 — each organization only after it has accepted its place and given permission to be named.
  • Acceptance: a selected organization has seven days from notification to accept. If it declines or cannot proceed, another applicant is selected using the same criteria.
  • Agreement: a signed pro bono services agreement is required before work begins.
  • Content: your text and photos are due within 14 days of your project kickoff.
  • Delivery: websites are built and handed over during the first quarter of 2027.

If you aren't selected

Five organizations receive a website; everyone who applies gets a respectful answer. If you are not selected, our Knowledge Center and other public resources remain open to you, and you are welcome to follow along or to subscribe if you choose to — that is always a separate, optional decision. Applying does not create any entitlement to free services, ongoing consulting, membership benefits, or selection in a future round.
