Digital media & brand development

Owned digital properties, search, content operations, and audience growth — run the way we run our own portfolio of brands.

If you're new to this

If your business or your creative work lives mostly on a social platform, you're renting. A website people can find, and an email list you control, are the part you own. We help you build that home, decide what to publish and how often so you can actually keep it up, and turn what you make into something people can buy. You don't need to know what SEO stands for.

If you do this for a living

Static-first sites on Git-backed hosting (Netlify or equivalent) with per-page metadata, canonical and structured data done at build; keyword and intent mapping before any page is written; a content operation designed to a stated weekly capacity with reuse paths across channels; email as the primary owned channel; product scoping (digital goods, print-on-demand merch, memberships) against observed audience behavior rather than assumptions. The stack and process are the ones running Rebel Herd's own properties.

Who it's for
Creators and niche publishers, small businesses that need a real home online instead of a rented social profile, and founders launching a brand who want it built on a foundation they own.
Problems we solve
  • Your entire audience lives on a platform that can change the rules tomorrow
  • A website that exists but doesn't do anything — no search traffic, no inquiries, no list
  • Content that gets made but never gets systematized, so it stops when you get busy
  • A brand idea that needs a name, a voice, a site, and a first product, in that order
What you receive
  • Brand foundation: positioning, voice, visual direction, and the words on the page
  • A fast, owned website on infrastructure you control, with the SEO basics done correctly from day one
  • A content operation: what gets made, how often, by whom, and how it's reused across channels
  • Audience systems: email list, search, and the handful of social channels that matter for you
  • Digital products — guides, templates, merchandise, memberships — scoped to what your audience will actually buy
Where this comes from
Rebel Herd Media runs its own portfolio of independent properties on the same stack and process we recommend to clients: ourfurlife.com, a character-driven storytelling brand, and corporatewitch.media, a professional brand, among others. These are owned projects, not client work, and they're shown as proof the process runs. When we say a workflow is sustainable for one person, it's because one person is sustaining it.
Typical scope & fee
A brand-and-site launch is quoted as a fixed project. Ongoing content operations and SEO are monthly retainers with a defined deliverable list, not an hours bucket.

Own the house, rent the billboard

Social platforms are billboards: useful for being seen, terrible as a place to live. Reach gets throttled, formats change, accounts get suspended for reasons no human will explain. A website and an email list are the house. Everything we build points traffic from the billboards back to the house, where the relationship — and the revenue — can be yours.

Search is still the quiet workhorse

For most small brands, search traffic is the most durable audience there is: people arriving because they were already looking for what you do. Getting it right isn't tricks; it's clear pages about specific things, built on a site that loads fast, with titles and structure that make sense to a person. That's where we start, and it's usually where the first measurable results come from.

Content you can keep up

The content plans that die are the ones designed for a team of five and handed to one person. We design the operation around the real capacity: what can be made in the time you have, how one piece becomes four, and what gets dropped first when the week goes sideways. A smaller plan that runs beats a bigger plan that stalls.
