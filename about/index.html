About the founder

Merry Grant spent nearly twenty years inside one commercial audio company, moving from the books to the customer to the product to the design table. Rebel Herd Media is the independent, woman-owned company she founded on that experience.

The thing she is best at is not on a spec sheet. For nearly two decades her job was to take a first-time buyer who didn't know a 70-volt line from a lamp cord and make them comfortable asking anything, and then, an hour later, to hold a detailed technical conversation with a consulting engineer who had specified a hundred systems. Both got the same respect and the same care. That is the founding principle of this company, and it is the reason the engineering below exists.

Merry joined a national commercial audio equipment and design company in 2007 in bookkeeping and stayed for nineteen and a half years. That tenure ran through customer service management, building and running the company's Amazon and eBay e-commerce channels, and finally a hybrid role as a senior sales and design engineer: designing sound masking, distributed audio, assistive-listening, and field PA systems, writing the quotes, and carrying the customer relationship from first call to commissioning.

The design work covered K-12 and higher education, federal and municipal government, healthcare, corporate offices, fitness, hospitality, and houses of worship — hundreds of rooms, from a single private office to multi-floor federal tenant build-outs to stadiums. Her specialty became speech privacy and sound masking, where she holds the Biamp Cambridge Sound Masking certification and designs both direct-field and in-plenum systems.

Alongside the engineering, she became the company's internal advocate for practical AI adoption: building AI-assisted quoting, pipeline, and design workflows for her own book of business, writing the safeguards before policy required them, and training colleagues on what the tools could and couldn't be trusted with. That experience — adoption from inside a working sales job, not from a conference stage — is the basis of the AI strategy practice.

The digital media practice comes from the other half of her working life. She has built Rebel Herd Media's own portfolio of independent brands — storytelling, humor, and professional properties — handling positioning, sites, content operations, and products herself. She consults on what she has personally shipped.

She holds a BBA from Sam Houston State University and lives in Dallas, Georgia.
