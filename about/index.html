Experience matters. How you treat people matters more.

Rebel Herd Media was founded by Merry Grant, an independent commercial audio designer and consultant with nearly twenty years of experience in technical sales, customer service, commercial audio design, and business operations.

Her work has included sound masking, speech privacy, distributed audio, paging, assistive listening, and audio system design for educational, commercial, healthcare, and government environments.

She understands the technical details. She also understands that customers should never have to master those details before asking for help. Some clients arrive with architectural drawings and detailed specifications. Others arrive with a problem they cannot quite explain. Both deserve the same attention.

That belief is central to Rebel Herd Media. Merry also brings practical experience in AI-assisted workflows, responsible technology adoption, digital publishing, and independent brand development. Her approach is straightforward: listen first, understand the objective, explain the options, and deliver work the customer can use.

Rebel Herd Media is independently owned and woman-owned.
