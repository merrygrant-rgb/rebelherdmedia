Privacy

Short version: the only information this site collects is what you type into the contact form, and it's used to reply to you.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email address, the organization you enter, the topic you choose, and your message. Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider, Netlify, and delivered to mgrant@rebelherdmedia.com.

What we do with it

We use it to answer your inquiry and, if you become a client, to do the work. We don't sell it, rent it, or add you to a marketing list. If you'd like your inquiry deleted, email mgrant@rebelherdmedia.com and we'll remove it.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies of its own and currently runs no analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may log a request from your browser; see Google's privacy policy for details. If we add analytics later, this page will say so first.
