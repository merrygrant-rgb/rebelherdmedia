Rebel Herd Media / Getting started

Tell us about the work.

A few questions so we understand what your people actually do before anyone says the word "workflow." About ten minutes.

You don't have to speak the language of AI to benefit from it. We meet you where you are, explain unfamiliar technology in terms you already understand, and build on the knowledge you bring. You don't have to learn our language. We'll start by speaking yours.

Answer in your own words. Skip anything you don't know; "not sure" is a perfectly good answer and tells us something useful. Please don't paste confidential drawings, plans, or restricted business information here. We'll set up a private place for those after we talk.

About you and your organization

Where are you starting from? There is no wrong answer. This only tells us which words to use.
Owner, manager, team lead, the person who got asked to "figure out AI"…
Plain description. "We quote HVAC jobs and chase invoices." "We run a dental office." "We write grant reports." The more ordinary, the more useful.

Where you are with AI

Tools, experiments, a pilot that fizzled, nothing at all. Honest answers help most.
What do people retype, re-summarize, re-format, or re-check? What's the task everyone dreads?
Even if it's vague. "Make the proposals faster." "Stop losing things in email." "Help the new person get up to speed."
Privacy, accuracy, the team not adopting it, leadership saying no, looking foolish, losing control of quality. Name it and we'll address it directly.
We build inside what you already have wherever we can.

Practical details

A busy season coming, a hire you're trying to avoid, a leadership deadline. Or "none."
Context, politics, the thing you'd only say on a call. Welcome here too.
