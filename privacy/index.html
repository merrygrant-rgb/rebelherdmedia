Privacy policy

Short version: this site collects what you type into its forms, uses it for the purpose you sent it, and never sells it. You can browse every page without submitting anything. The only marketing list we keep is one you join on purpose and confirm by email. Page-view analytics are aggregate and cookie-free.

Draft — pending COO wording review. This policy describes the site as it will operate once the current update is published; final wording is subject to approval.

What we collect, and why

Each form collects only what it shows you. Nothing is collected in the background.

  • Contact form (/contact/): your name, email address, organization (optional), the topic you choose, and your message. Used to reply to you and, if we go on to work together, to do the work.
  • Project intake forms (/intake/audio/, /intake/ai/, /intake/brand/): the project details you enter — contact information, organization, and the room, workflow, or brand questions on that form. Used to scope and prepare for a consultation you've requested.
  • Rebel Herd Initiative application (/herd/): your name, business or nonprofit name, email, city and state, organization type, website (optional), your answers about your mission and how a website would help, your readiness timeline, your acceptance of the official terms, and — separately and optionally — whether you permit public recognition if selected. Used only to run the initiative: eligibility, selection against the published criteria, notifying applicants, and (with that separate permission) introducing selected organizations publicly.
  • Knowledge Center updates signup: your email address, first name (optional), and which topics you'd like. Used only to send the emails you asked for.

Consent record. When a form includes the optional marketing box and you tick it, we store with your submission that you did, the date and time, and the version of the wording you agreed to. Leaving the box unticked never affects your inquiry, intake, or application.

Marketing emails — opt-in and double opt-in only

We send occasional emails — Knowledge Center articles, practical business tips, company news, educational resources, and special offers — only to people who asked for them. Ticking a box starts the process; you are not on the list until you click the confirmation link we email you (double opt-in). If you never click it, you are never added. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect immediately. We never add anyone because they wrote to us, applied to the Rebel Herd Initiative, or booked a consultation, and we never sell, rent, or share the list.

Who handles your information

We use a small number of service providers that process information on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Netlify hosts this website, runs the code that validates form submissions, and stores every form submission (including the consent record described above).
  • Google Workspace provides our email. Form notifications and anything you send to info@rebelherdmedia.com are received there.
  • Brevo is our email service provider. It stores the email address, first name, topic preferences, and consent details of people who confirm a marketing subscription, sends those emails and the confirmation link, and sends the one-time confirmation email to Rebel Herd Initiative applicants. Brevo does not receive your other form answers.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics gives us aggregate page-view counts (pages visited, referring sites, country, browser type). It does not use cookies, does not store anything in your browser, does not fingerprint devices, and does not identify individual visitors.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces; your browser requests the font files from Google when a page loads, which Google may log.

No advertising networks, social-media pixels, or other third-party scripts run on this site.

Retention

Proposed schedule — pending final legal review and verification of our deletion procedures.

  • Inquiries and intake forms are kept for as long as we need them to respond to you, to carry out any work you engage us for, and to maintain ordinary business records.
  • Rebel Herd Initiative applications that are not selected are kept for 24 months after the November 28, 2026 closing date.
  • Judging and selection records are kept for 24 months after the closing date.
  • Selected organizations' applications and project records are kept for 24 months after the project is completed, unless a longer retention or preservation requirement applies under law.
  • Public recognition permission: we keep the consent record for as long as recognition content is in use, and we document any withdrawal and act on it.
  • Confirmed marketing subscriptions are kept while the subscription remains active, subject to your withdrawal, deletion requests, and applicable requirements.
  • Unconfirmed marketing requests: if you start a signup but never click the confirmation link, you are never added to the list. The unconfirmed request remains in our form records until it is deleted under our retention procedures or you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics are aggregate from the start; there is no personal record to delete.

Cookies, analytics, and tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Our analytics are cookie-free and aggregate, as described above, which is why there is no consent banner. If we ever introduce anything that requires consent, this page will be updated first and that technology will not run before you agree where the law requires it.

Your choices and requests

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw marketing consent or recognition permission by emailing info@rebelherdmedia.com. We honor deletion requests subject to applicable legal and record-keeping exceptions — for example, records we must keep to document a promotion or a completed project — and we'll tell you what we've done and, if we must keep something, why. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law; we'll honor requests as the law requires.

Drawings and project files

Please don't paste confidential drawings, architectural plans, or restricted business information into a form. Once we're working together, we set up a private file-sharing location specific to your project.

Children

This site is intended for adults and organizations. The Rebel Herd Initiative requires applicants to be 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from children.
