Privacy policy

Short version: the only information this site collects is what you type into the contact form, and it's used to reply to you. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email address, the organization you enter (optional), the topic you choose, and your message. The site doesn't collect anything else, and you can browse every page without submitting anything.

How inquiries are used

We use your inquiry to respond to it and, if we go on to work together, to do the work. We don't sell or rent your information, share it with third parties for their marketing, or add you to a mailing list because you wrote to us.

Who handles it

  • Netlify hosts this website and processes and stores form submissions.
  • Google Workspace provides our email, where form notifications and direct emails to info@rebelherdmedia.com are received.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces; your browser requests the font files from Google when a page loads, which Google may log. No other third-party scripts, embeds, or services run on this site.

Retention

We keep inquiries and related correspondence for as long as we need them to respond to you, to carry out any work you engage us for, and to maintain ordinary business records. You can ask us to delete an inquiry at any time (below).

Cookies, analytics, and tracking

This site sets no cookies, uses no local storage, and runs no analytics, advertising pixels, or embedded third-party media. Because there is no nonessential tracking, there is no consent banner. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first and any such technology will not run before you consent where consent is required.

Your choices and requests

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing info@rebelherdmedia.com. We'll confirm what we've done. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law; we'll honor requests as the law requires.

Drawings and project files

Please don't paste confidential drawings, architectural plans, or restricted business information into the contact form. Once we're working together, we set up a private file-sharing location specific to your project.

Children

This site is intended for adults and organizations. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Rebel Herd Media LLC, Dallas, Georgia. info@rebelherdmedia.com.

Last updated October 2026. This policy describes the site as it currently operates and will be updated if that changes.
