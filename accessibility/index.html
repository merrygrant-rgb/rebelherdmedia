Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone. If something doesn't, tell us and we'll fix it.

What we've done

  • Every page can be navigated by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a skip-to-content link.
  • Text and background colors are checked against WCAG 2.1 contrast ratios: body text meets AAA, links and buttons meet AA, and large accent text meets AA for large text.
  • Form fields have visible labels tied to the inputs, required fields are marked, and the browser reports what's missing before the form submits.
  • Pages use a single H1 and an ordered heading structure.
  • The site has no autoplaying media, no motion effects, and respects reduced-motion settings.
  • Layouts are tested at phone width and don't scroll sideways.

What we haven't claimed

We've built and checked the site against WCAG 2.1 principles, but we haven't had it independently audited and we don't claim full ADA or WCAG conformance. Decorative graphics are marked as such; the one illustrative graphic on the homepage has a text description.

Tell us

If you have trouble using any part of this site, email info@rebelherdmedia.com with the page and what happened. We'll respond and work to fix it.
